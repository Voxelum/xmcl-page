# XMCL 이야기

## The story of XMCL

I still remember one evening in high school. A friend and I wanted to play a modpack together. At the time, there were few launchers that made installing mods easy. I used Windows, my friend used a Mac, and I had barely played Minecraft on macOS. Installing Forge alone produced problem after problem.

Three hours passed troubleshooting remotely. By the time the game was finally installed, the virtual LAN software would not connect. After spending the entire evening troubleshooting, my friend had lost all enthusiasm and gave up.

That night, I quietly made myself a promise: one day I would build a launcher that would solve the problem of installing modpacks and playing them with friends.

## Built for people who want to make it work

XMCL has always aimed to serve players who love modpacks, care about how their game works, and want to share it with friends without needing to become networking or server administration experts.

Whether you choose peer multiplayer or rent an always-available server, the goal is the same: **less troubleshooting, and an earlier arrival in the same world with your friends.**
