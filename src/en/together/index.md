# XMCL Together

XMCL is a free and open-source launcher that works without an XMCL Together account.

XMCL Together is a separate, optional set of online services operated by CI010. Together Home provides AI-assisted troubleshooting and relay connectivity for groups playing modpacks together. It is currently offered for **$2.99 per month**, paid from your XMCL Together balance.

- The launcher remains free and open source.
- An account is required only for Together services.
- Together does not include a copy or license of Minecraft.
- You must obtain Minecraft and third-party content lawfully.
